# RCM Co-Op Succession plan

The departure of a [committee member or senior member of the RCM Co-op](committees.md), whether planned or emergency, can cause disruption to work and affect the short- and long-term continuity.

This policy sets out the procedure in the case of planned or emergency departure of any of the named members below, including how vacant positions will be filled:
- Governance and Advisory Committee member;
- Co-Op director; 
- Senior member of the Co-Op;
- Co-op staff member

## 1. Term length and selection of Chair
1.1 Term length in RCM Co-Op Committee roles:
- Incorporates fresh perspectives; 
- Prevents stagnation of power and practices;
- Creates opportunities for development into leadership; 
- Sets expectations and accountabiltiy;
- Distributes responsibility.

1.2 There is a three year term limit for members of the RCM Co-op Governance and Advisory committee. 
Term length for all other committees will be decided and documented as the committees are formed. 

1.3 At the end of the term, there can be a vote to extend the term for a maximum of 3 years.

1.4 Where multiple terms are concluding simultaneously, there should be a staggered off boarding to retain knowledge of processes and current work.

1.5 Members can step down at any time before the end of their term, following the offboarding process.

1.6 The Chair of each committee will be determined by the processed described in [the decision making policy](./policy-decision-making.md).
The Chair will sit for a maximum 3 year term, as described above. 

## 2. Time commitment
2.2 Governance and Advisory Committee: 1 meeting every 6 months (2 x year) and extraordinary meetings when needed for decision making purposes. 

2.1 Core Team: Monthly meeting of 1 hour.
This may increase to bi-monthly meetings when funded projects and packages of work are happening.

2.2 The time commitment required before and after the meeting may vary based on tasks undertaken or actions agreed by the members of the committee and core team.

## 3. Legal status
3.1 Roles on committees are voluntary.

3.2 Honoraria can be provided, when funds are available, through recommendation by the committee Chair to be agreed by the core team. 

3.3 An accessibility fund will be made available, when possible, to ensure that Committee members and core team can participate in carrying out their responsibilities. 

## 4. Offboarding process for governance committee and core team
4.1 Members must send an email to the chair of the committee and Director of core team to notify them of the intention to step down from a committee role. 

4.2 Chair will send an email back suggesting a time period when they can be off-boarded from the committee.

4.3 Members must document any current work they are doing for the committee and/or core team and share this with the committee and core team. 

## 5. Process when unable to contact committee member or core team member
5.1 The committee Chair and Core Team Director will keep a list of members, including contact details, regularly updated and shared with OLS.

5.2 If there is no response from the nominated contact (Chair of committee or and Director of core team) and OLS has contacted committee members and core team members with no response for three months, 50% of the any money held by OLS will revert to OLS, and 100% after 6 months of inactivity.

## 6. Recruitment of new core team members
6.1 Current committee members and core team members will identify potential co-op members to invite to the core team.

6.2 The committee and core team will agree on the names of who to invite and then the committee chair will send invitation emails. 

6.3 New core team members will then be onboarded by current core team members to explain co-op set up, documentation, processes and current work.

6.4 Requests to join the core team will be discussed with all members and voted on.

## 7. Recruitment of new committee members
7.1 Current core team and co-op members will be asked to volunteer to be on the committee. 

7.2 If a core team or co-op member comes forward, the committee board will vote to appoint that member or not.

7.3 If no one volunteers, then the committee will identify co-op members or external candidates and vote on who to invite. 

7.4 The committee chair will then invite this person and they will be onboarded by the chair of the committee. 

## 8. Decision making on core team
8.1 At core team meetings, at least 3 core team members need to be present to make decisions.

8.2 If too few members attend the meeting, the meeting is to be rearranged at the earliest availability of at least 3 members. If this starts to happen regularly, the chair/director must speak to the member/s involved and a new member will be recruited, if needed.

8.3 A majority vote is enough to go ahead with proposed action.

8.4 The decisions must be recorded in the meeting notes.

## 9. Decision making on committee
9.1 At committee meetings, at least 3 members need to be present to make decisions by voting. This must include the chair, and when recruited, one external member. 

9.2 If too few committee members attend the meeting, the meeting is to be rearranged at the earliest availability of at least 3 members.  If this starts to happen regularly, the chair must speak to the member/s involved and a new member will be recruited, if needed.

9.3 Decisions should be recorded in meeting notes.
