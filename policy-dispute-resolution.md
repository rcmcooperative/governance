# Dispute Resolution Policy

## 1. Introduction

The [Governance and Advisory Committee](./committees.md#governance-and-advisory-committee) of RCM Co-op is committed to reaching a prompt and fair resolution of any disputes, conflicts, or disagreements that may arise from time to time, and that may threaten the functioning of the board.

## 2. Scope

This policy refers to disputes: 
- Between Directors; 
- By a Director regarding a Governance and Advisory Committee policy, process or procedure; 
- By a Director regarding a resolution of the Governance and Advisory Committee;
- Between Governance and Advisory Committee members including the Chair;
- Between RCM Co-Op leadership;

This policy may be reviewed as the wider process for [decision making](./policy-decision-making.md) is developed as appropriate for the size and activities of the Co-Op.


## 3. Policy

The RCM Co-op Governance and Advisory Committee encourages Directors to resolve any issues or concerns that they may have at the earliest opportunity. 
It is important that as issues do arise, they are dealt with in a fair and timely manner. 
While some conflicts will be resolved by an informal discussion between the parties, others will need a process for successful resolution.

In dealing with disputes, the Governance and Advisory Committee endorsees the following principles:

- Respect for another’s point of view;
- Commitment to resolving the issue;
- Willingness to compromise;
- Confidentiality;
- Impartiality;
- Respect;
- Prompt action; and, 
- Freedom from repercussions.

These principals should be operationalised in line with the procedure detailed in [Section 4. Procedure](#4-procedure)

## 4. Procedure

4.1. The dispute must be set out in writing and sent to the chair. 
The chair must acknowledge receipt of this document within two days.

4.1.1 Where the dispute involves the chair, it must be sent to the chair and one other member of the [Governance and Advisory Committee](./committees.md#governance-and-advisory-committee). 
Both the chair and additional Governance and Advisory Committee member must acknowledge receipt of this document within two days.

4.2. The chair will use their discretion to bring the issue to the next Governance and Advisory Committee meeting or call an extraordinary meeting.

4.3. When raised at the committee meeting all people involved in the dispute will be given the right to speak.

4.4. The matter should be discussed with all Directors present, unless they have advised the chair, preferably in writing, that they are aware there is a dispute resolution meeting being held and they are unable to attend.

4.5. The chair will call for a motion from the committee, e.g. to appoint an independent assessor, seek mediation, call a special general meeting, or to dismiss the complaint. 
All Directors present at the meeting will vote on the motion. 
The decision on the motion will be implemented by the procedure described in [the decision making policy](./policy-decision-making.md)

4.6. A committee decision may be reviewed in situations where:
- New information has emerged that was not available when the original decision was made.
- The committee has become aware of an error in previous information that was used to make the decision.
- A Director did not feel able to present their case at the time the committee made its decision.

## 5. Mediation
5.1. Where mediation is sought, the mediator must be:
a) a person chosen by agreement between the parties; or
b) in the absence of agreement, a person appointed by the Governance and Advisory Committee.

5.2. A member of the Governance and Advisory Committee can be a mediator, but may not be a member who is a party to the dispute.

5.3. The parties to the dispute must, in good faith, attempt to settle the dispute by mediation.

5.4. The mediator, in conducting the mediation, must:
a) give the parties to the mediation process every opportunity to be heard; and
b) allow due consideration by all parties of any written statement submitted by any party; and
c) ensure that natural justice is accorded to the parties to the dispute throughout the mediation process.

5.5. The mediator must not determine the dispute.

5.6. The mediation must be confidential and without prejudice.

5.7. If the mediation process does not result in the dispute being resolved, the parties may seek to resolve the dispute otherwise in the board or at law.


## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

It is the responsibility of the Chair to ensure that:
- Committee members are aware of this policy;
- Disputes are handled respectfully, confidentially, and in accordance with natural justice.
